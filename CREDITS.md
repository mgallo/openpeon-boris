# Audio credits

Audio: **Boris**, the Italian television series. All audio rights remain with
their respective owners; this unofficial fan pack does not claim ownership
or grant a new license to the recordings.

MP3 files and clip labels come from [cedmax/boris-soundboard](https://github.com/cedmax/boris-soundboard),
pinned to commit `d1f08163d42f01efccbdcdb4e16bc24882d36130`.
The files in `sounds/` are unchanged copies of the identically named files in
that repository's `public/mp3/` directory. SHA-256 digests are in `openpeon.json`.

No audio license was supplied in that source tree. This pack therefore omits
the optional manifest license field rather than assigning a license to third-party
recordings. Establish redistribution rights before publishing to a registry.

Contains explicit Italian language. Labels identify excerpts, not necessarily
the full dialogue. René, Duccio, Stanis, Biascica, Martellone, Sergio, Arianna,
and Karin provide your new terminal notifications.
